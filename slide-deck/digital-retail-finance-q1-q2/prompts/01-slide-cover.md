## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: Clean digital precision with crisp structured layouts. Professional navy and gold palette projecting competence and institutional credibility. Balanced information density with strategic whitespace.

Background:
  Texture: None — crisp digital clarity, pure white base
  Base Color: Pure White (#FFFFFF) with navy structural elements

Typography:
  Headlines: Bold geometric sans-serif with perfect circular O shapes, semi-bold weight, navy color
  Body: Humanist sans-serif, regular weight, comfortable line height, dark gray

Color Palette:
  Primary Text: Navy (#1E3A5F) - headlines, key text
  Background: Pure White (#FFFFFF) - main slide background
  Accent 1: Gold (#C9A227) - premium highlights, KPI emphasis
  Accent 2: Light Navy (#3D5A80) - secondary elements, dividers
  Success: Corporate Green (#059669) - positive metrics
  Neutral: Light Gray (#F3F4F6) - background sections, cards

Visual Elements:
  - Clean structured grid layouts
  - Minimal decorative geometry (thin gold lines, corner accents)
  - Professional outlined iconography

Density Guidelines:
  - Content per slide: 3-4 key elements max
  - Whitespace: Generous margins, breathing room

Style Rules:
  Do: Maintain clear visual hierarchy; use gold for emphasis; align to grid
  Don't: Use playful elements; apply heavy decorative effects; crowd slide
